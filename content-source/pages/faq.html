All data processed through Rainbow is hosted exclusively in France, on infrastructure operated under French and European law. This means your data is fully subject to GDPR, entirely outside the scope of the US Cloud Act, and never accessible to foreign government authorities without going through European legal channels. Unlike Microsoft Teams or Google Workspace, whose data centers in Europe remain operated by US-headquartered parent companies, Rainbow's infrastructure has no jurisdictional ambiguity. Your data is where you think it is, governed by the law you expect.

No. Your conversations, meeting summaries, documents, and communications are never used to train Rainbow's AI models or any external model. This is a structural guarantee, not a contractual opt-out. The AI processes your data to generate outputs for you, summaries, transcriptions, translations, and that data stays within your environment. This is one of the most significant differences between Rainbow AI and US-based AI tools like Microsoft Copilot or Google Gemini, where data usage policies are more ambiguous and subject to change.

Yes, natively and by design. Rainbow was built around European data protection requirements from the ground up, not retrofitted to comply with them after the fact. This includes full GDPR compliance, compatibility with CNIL guidelines, and architecture that supports HDS certification requirements for healthcare organisations. For organisations in regulated sectors, healthcare, legal, financial services, public sector, Rainbow's compliance posture is not an additional feature to configure. It is the default state of the product.

The US Cloud Act, passed in 2018, grants American federal authorities the legal power to compel US-headquartered technology companies to produce data stored anywhere in the world, including on European servers. If your organisation uses Teams, Zoom, Slack, or Google Workspace, your data is technically accessible to US authorities under this framework, without you being notified. For companies handling sensitive client data, patient records, legal documents, or strategic business information, this is an active compliance risk, not a theoretical one. Rainbow operates entirely outside this framework. There is no US parent company, no Cloud Act exposure, and no scenario in which your data can be accessed by a foreign government without your knowledge.

Rainbow is ISO 27001 certified and holds European certifications such as CAN in Italy, C5 in Germany, ENS in Spain, and CSPN from France's ANSSI.

The platform is designed with a data privacy focus, following applicable law such as GDPR and CCPA.

Rainbow AI is launching in September 2025 with a suite of native AI capabilities designed for professional environments. These include automatic meeting summaries delivered immediately after every call, real-time translation across all video conversations, voice-to-structured-text transcription for field and mobile workers, AI-assisted professional writing within the messaging interface, intelligent message prioritization for high-volume communication environments, and automated follow-up tracking from meeting commitments. All AI features are active from the first conversation, require no configuration, and are available to every user regardless of company size, from a solo practitioner to a 10 000 person organisation.

Yes, and this is one of Rainbow's most significant technical differentiators. Through its open API and SDK architecture, Rainbow integrates directly into the tools your teams already use, rather than requiring your organisation to restructure around it. This means a financial advisor can launch a video call from within their banking application. A doctor can trigger a teleconsultation from a patient record. A logistics coordinator can receive automatically structured incident reports from field workers' voice messages, all within existing workflows, without switching applications. If your organisation has specific integration requirements, Rainbow's business partner programme includes dedicated technical support for custom integrations.

Yes, and this is a deliberate design choice. Many enterprise communication tools, Teams in particular, are optimised for office-based, desk-bound workers with reliable internet connections and full-specification computers. Rainbow's mobile application is built for field environments: lightweight, low-bandwidth, and fully functional on standard smartphones. Voice transcription, message prioritisation, and AI-generated structured reports are specifically designed for workers who communicate verbally on site and need documentation to happen automatically, without typing, without desk access, and without switching between multiple applications.

Yes. Rainbow's SDK allows organisations to embed communication capabilities, video calls, messaging, AI features, directly into their own applications, creating branded experiences that keep clients within the organisation's own digital environment. This is particularly relevant for financial institutions that want to offer adviser video calls within their banking app, healthcare providers embedding teleconsultation within patient portals, and any organisation that wants to provide branded communication without directing clients to a third-party tool. This level of native embedding is not available in Teams or Zoom without significant custom development investment.

AI and machine learning use the information gathered to support informed decisions and trigger alerts or recommendations before an event occurs.

Yes, and unlike most enterprise communication tools, Rainbow does not impose minimum seat counts, complex onboarding requirements, or pricing structures that only make sense at scale. A five-person legal practice, a three-practitioner medical cabinet, or a solo consultant with a small client-facing team accesses the same AI capabilities, the same data sovereignty guarantees, and the same integration possibilities as a multinational corporation. The only difference is the number of users paying. There is no feature dilution, no SMB-tier product with reduced capabilities, and no hidden upgrade path required to access the full Rainbow experience.

For most organisations, Rainbow is operational within a single working day. There is no server infrastructure to configure, no lengthy IT project to manage, and no training programme required before teams can begin using it productively. Because Rainbow's AI features activate within existing conversations rather than requiring new behaviours, adoption happens organically from day one. For larger organisations with specific integration requirements, embedding Rainbow into existing applications via API, for instance, deployment timelines vary based on the complexity of the integration, and Rainbow's technical team provides dedicated support throughout the process.

Your data belongs to you, entirely and without condition. Upon termination of your Rainbow subscription, you retain full access to export all your data, conversations, files, AI-generated summaries, and any other content generated within the platform, in standard, portable formats. Rainbow does not retain your data after the termination period defined in your contract, and no data is archived for Rainbow's own purposes. This is a direct reflection of the sovereignty principle that governs the entire product: your data was never Rainbow's to keep.
